Privacy Policy

AB Fencing Academy app, built by Loopify. Last updated: October 20, 2026

This app is used by AB Fencing Academy at Club One, Maadi, Cairo, to manage training schedules, attendance, fees and messages for its athletes, parents and coaches. This page explains what information the app uses and why.

What we collect

  • Account details: name, email address and phone number. The academy creates your account.
  • Athlete details: year of birth, height, weight and fencing weapon, entered by the athlete or the parent.
  • Training records: which trainings an athlete attended, arrival and leaving times, and the weekly ranking.
  • Payments: you choose InstaPay or cash. With InstaPay, the app opens InstaPay, you pay there, and then you send proof of the transfer in the app: the transaction reference and a screenshot. The app does not handle your bank or card details.
  • Messages: the messages between athletes, parents and coaches. The head coach, who runs the academy, can read all messages.
  • Notification token: a code that lets us send notifications to your phone.
  • Location (coaches only): coaches can allow location access so the app records when they arrive at and leave the academy. Athletes and parents are never tracked.
  • Crash reports: technical information when the app fails, so we can fix it. It does not include your messages or payments.

Why we use it

Only to run the academy: show schedules, record attendance, collect fees, send reminders and announcements, let people message coaches, and calculate coach working hours. We do not sell your information and we do not show advertising.

Children

Young athletes use the app through a parent account. The academy creates each child's profile and links it to the parent's account. Parents can see and edit their child's details (name, photo, sizes and an emergency contact). Photos shown to visitors are training photos chosen by the academy.

Who processes the data for us

  • Supabase stores the database and uploaded files.
  • Expo delivers push notifications.
  • Sentry receives crash reports.

Keeping it safe

Every person can only see the information that belongs to them or their children. Staff see what their job needs. Connections to the servers are encrypted.

Your choices

  • You can edit athlete details in the app under More.
  • You can delete your account in the app under More, then Delete my account. This removes your account and personal data. The academy may keep payment records it needs for its accounts.
  • You can also ask the academy to delete or export your data using the contact below.
